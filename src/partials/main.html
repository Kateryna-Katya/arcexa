Jelly Power System

Power That Sticks

Every Jelly symbol can enter an empowered state after repeated successful drops.
Once empowered, jellies act as flexible Wild elements and remain active while the flow continues.
The longer the sequence lasts, the more of the board becomes energized.

Scatter Energy Events

Moments That Change the Pace

When several Scatter symbols appear together, a special energy phase begins.
At the start of this phase one Jelly character is randomly highlighted and its power level increases based on the number of Scatters involved.
This creates variety and personality in every major moment.

Multiplier Flow

Growing Impact

Each Jelly carries its own impact value.
When that Jelly contributes to a successful outcome, its current value is applied. During energy phases, Scatter symbols further increase this value, allowing the impact to grow step by step instead of resetting instantly.
