JELLY BOOM

Seriously, nothing sits still in this game - everything's constantly bouncing and wobbling around.

Okay, so picture this - literally everything in the game just won't sit still. It's all wobbling and bouncing around like crazy.
Jelly Boom drops you into a playful universe ruled by four tiny jellies who love motion, color, and chain reactions. Every action sends ripples across the board. The longer the flow continues, the stronger the jelly energy becomes.
